About Tieyoh

Tieyoh is transforming digital gifting by connecting people, merchants, and payments through a unified merchant-centric platform.

Rather than simply transferring money or purchasing isolated gift cards, Tieyoh enables consumers to send purposeful gifts that remain connected to the merchants, brands, or locations they were intended for — while giving merchants new ways to engage customers and participate in a modern digital economy.

Built on intelligent merchant identity, digital wallets, and flexible payment infrastructure, Tieyoh bridges the gap between traditional payment networks and closed-loop commerce, creating a scalable ecosystem that benefits consumers, merchants, sponsor banks, and payment partners alike.

Our long-term vision extends beyond gifting. Tieyoh is building the merchant identity and payment infrastructure that will enable loyalty, rewards, merchant-funded offers, stored value, and merchant-specific financial services — all through a single platform designed to simplify how people give, spend, and connect with businesses they value.

Today's gifting and merchant payment ecosystems are fragmented, expensive, and difficult to scale. Consumers manage dozens of physical and digital gift cards, each tied to a single merchant, each carrying independent balances that cannot be consolidated or easily managed. Small and medium-sized merchants face significant barriers when launching digital gift card programs due to lengthy commercial negotiations, acquiring processor integrations, payment network dependencies, and technology costs.

Tieyoh bridges these gaps by creating a single merchant-centric platform where gifting, payments, merchant engagement, and customer experiences work together within one ecosystem — interactive, engaging, rewarding, and simple for both consumers and merchants, with lower fees.

Guiding Principles

  1. Purpose Before Payments — every gift should preserve the sender's intent while allowing recipients the flexibility to enjoy meaningful experiences with their favorite merchants.
  2. Merchant Accessibility — digital gifting should not require months of commercial negotiations or complex payment network implementations.
  3. One Wallet, One Experience — all merchant gifts are consolidated into a single virtual prepaid card, with balances tracked in a central ledger.
  4. Meaningful Social Connections — an optional, privacy-controlled social layer turns gifting into a collaborative, relationship-centered experience.
